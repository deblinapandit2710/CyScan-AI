How the deep learning pipeline works and how to hook this web interface to your real PyTorch/TensorFlow backend.

ML Pipeline Architecture

1. Satellite Channel Preprocessing Extract Infrared (IR 10.8µm) and Water Vapor (6.7µm) channels from INSAT/GOES HDF5 or NetCDF4 files. Apply radiometric calibration and brightness temperature conversion.
2. Eye & Vortex Center Localization (YOLOv8) A bounding box regression model identifies the eye or Central Dense Overcast (CDO) center with pixel precision (x, y, radius).
3. Intensity & Wind Estimation (Deep Dvorak / EfficientNet) A convolutional regression network estimates maximum sustained wind speed (MSW in knots) and Minimum Central Pressure (MCP in hPa) from cloud banding pattern features.
4. IMD / Saffir-Simpson Classification Outputs categorized alerts: Depression, Deep Depression, Cyclonic Storm, Severe, Very Severe, Extremely Severe, or Super Cyclone.
